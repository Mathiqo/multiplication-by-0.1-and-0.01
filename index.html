🌟 Let's Learn: Tenths

Imagine you have one whole chocolate bar.

We divide the whole into 10 equal parts.

The blue part is 1 part out of 10.

1/10 = 0.1

💡 Remember

1/10 means one part when a whole is divided into ten equal parts.

In decimal form:

1/10 = 0.1

🔍 Look at these examples

Two parts out of ten:

2/10 = 0.2

Five parts out of ten:

5/10 = 0.5

Ten parts out of ten:

10/10 = 1
