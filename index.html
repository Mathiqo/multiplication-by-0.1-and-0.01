What happens when we split one whole into smaller equal parts?

🍫 Imagine one whole chocolate bar.

🧩 Meet One Tenth

Click one piece.

👆 Choose one piece.
Step 3 of 12

🔢 One Tenth = 0.1

One part out of ten is:

1/10 = 0.1
1/10 is the fraction.

0.1 is the decimal.
💡 They represent exactly the same amount.
Step 4 of 12

🌈 See Tenths Grow

1/10 = 0.1
2/10 = 0.2
5/10 = 0.5
10/10 = 1
The digit after the decimal tells us how many tenths we have.
Step 5 of 12

🔲 Now Think Smaller

What if we divide a whole into 100 equal parts?

👆 Choose one tiny square.

Step 6 of 12

🔎 Meet One Hundredth

1/100 = 0.01
One tenth:
0.1

One hundredth:
0.01
💡 0.01 is smaller than 0.1.
Step 7 of 12

✖️ Let's Multiply by 0.1

Start with:

25 × 0.1
Multiplying by 0.1 is the same as dividing by 10.

25 ÷ 10 = 2.5
So...

25 × 0.1 = 2.5
Step 8 of 12

👀 Watch the Decimal Point Move

When we multiply by 0.1, the decimal point moves one place to the left.

2 5 . 0
← ONE PLACE LEFT
2 . 5
✖️ 0.1 → move the decimal point 1 place LEFT

Step 10 of 12

✖️ Now Multiply by 0.01

Let's start with:

25 × 0.01
Multiplying by 0.01 means dividing by 100.

25 ÷ 100 = 0.25
Therefore:

25 × 0.01 = 0.25
Step 11 of 12

👀 Watch It Move Two Places

× 0.01 means the decimal point moves two places left.

2 5 . 0 0
← TWO PLACES LEFT
0 . 2 5
✖️ 0.01 → move the decimal point 2 places LEFT
Step 12 of 12

🚀 You've Got It!

0.1 → one place left

0.01 → two places left

× 0.1 → ÷ 10

× 0.01 → ÷ 100
