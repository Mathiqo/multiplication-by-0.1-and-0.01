🌟 Step 1: Understanding Tenths

Imagine one whole chocolate bar.

We divide it into 10 equal parts.

One part out of ten is:

1/10 = 0.1
💡 One tenth = 0.1

🔎 Step 2: Understanding Hundredths

Now imagine a whole divided into 100 equal parts.

One part out of 100 is called one hundredth.

1/100 = 0.01
💡 One hundredth = 0.01

Notice the difference:

1/10 = 0.1
1/100 = 0.01

✖️ Step 3: Multiplying by 0.1

Multiplying a number by 0.1 is the same as dividing it by 10.

10 × 0.1 = 1
25 × 0.1 = 2.5
48 × 0.1 = 4.8
💡 × 0.1 means ÷ 10

✖️ Step 4: Multiplying by 0.01

Multiplying a number by 0.01 is the same as dividing it by 100.

10 × 0.01 = 0.1
25 × 0.01 = 0.25
48 × 0.01 = 0.48
💡 × 0.01 means ÷ 100

🧠 Remember

× 0.1 → divide by 10

× 0.01 → divide by 100

The decimal point moves according to the number of zeros in the denominator.
